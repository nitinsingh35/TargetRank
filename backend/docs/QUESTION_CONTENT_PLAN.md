# TargetRank Question Content Plan

This document establishes the strategic design, scale targets, distribution curves, and source provenance guidelines for the TargetRank question bank.

---

## 📈 Phased Scale Targets

We implement a phased content acquisition plan to avoid low-quality automated imports and ensure high-accuracy explanations.

### Stage A — Launch-Ready (Current Target)
*   **Topic Coverage**: 100 to 300 original practice questions per high-priority topic.
*   **PYQ Simulator**: 25 to 100 verified PYQs per available exam and topic.
*   **Current Affairs**: 20 to 50 current affairs questions per monthly pack.
*   **Daily Engagement**: 10 to 30 questions per daily quiz.

### Stage B — Strong Practice Library
*   **Topic Coverage**: 500 to 1,500 questions per high-demand topic.
*   **Difficulty**: Balanced across multiple difficulty bands.
*   **PYQ Coverage**: Comprehensive year-wise and topic-wise PYQs.
*   **Mock Tests**: At least 5 unique full-length mock tests per exam.

### Stage C — Large-Scale Question Bank
*   **Topic Coverage**: 2,000 to 5,000 questions reserved exclusively for high-weightage/volume topics (e.g., Parliament in Polity, Mechanics in Physics).
*   **Features**: Heavy use of quality review filters, automated deduplication checks, and regular static content updates.

---

## 📊 Question Distributions

To optimize exam preparation, we follow strict distribution curves for difficulty levels and question sources.

### 1. Difficulty Level Distribution
For any standard practice deck or custom exam configuration:
*   **20% Easy Foundation**: Basic conceptual checks, direct definitions.
*   **50% Medium Exam-Level**: Standard conceptual questions that mimic actual exam difficulty.
*   **25% Hard/High-Level**: Deep analytical questions requiring multi-statement reasoning.
*   **5% Very Difficult/Tricky**: Extremely challenging questions targeting outlier topics or complex calculations.

### 2. Question Source Distribution
To provide diverse practice paths:
*   **Original Concept Practice**: Target key syllabus points designed by subject matter experts.
*   **Verified Official PYQs**: Directly sourced from UPSC/BPSC/SSC CGL official papers.
*   **Current Affairs**: Sourced from daily national news portals, government reports, and international alerts.
*   **State-specific GK**: Focused Bihar (BPSC), Jharkhand (JPSC), and Uttar Pradesh (UPPSC) historical and administrative questions.
*   **Mock-Test-Only Questions**: Fresh unseen questions kept aside specifically for test-day simulators.

---

## ⚖️ Intellectual Property & Copyright Guidelines

To ensure the platform remains fully compliant, all content developers must adhere to the following rules:

1.  **No Scraping**: Never scrap or copy copyrighted materials, questions, or explanations from other coaching institutes or prep apps.
2.  **Official PYQ Provenance**: For Previous Year Questions, import only public-domain questions officially released by government exam commissions (UPSC, state PCS, SSC, etc.). Store the official source URL/reference alongside each question.
3.  **Concept-Only Custom Questions**: When creating practice questions based on textbooks (e.g., Laxmikanth's Polity, NCERTs), write original practice questions based on the concepts. Do not copy sentences directly from the book text.
4.  **Clear Attribution**: Label every question's copyright status (e.g., `original`, `public_domain`, `licensed`) and source reference clearly.
